ABOUT COMPANY

KGC Private Limited

KGC Private Limited (KGCPL) is one of India's premier Plant Efficiency & Quality Solutions consulting firms. Since our establishment in 2008, we have been delivering measurable, result-oriented industrial consultancy across a wide range of processing and manufacturing industries. We specialize in operational audits, process engineering, and efficiency advisory for sectors including food and beverages, pharmaceuticals (API), cosmetics, chemicals, FMCG, agro-processing, marine, dairy, meat processing, and general manufacturing industries.

Our core consulting practice is strictly focused on solving manufacturing bottlenecks, eliminating profit leaks, and creating self-managed factory operations through our Main Consulting Services: Cost Control Audits (reducing direct factory operating costs, eliminating hidden expenses, and controlling material variance); Production Planning & Control - PPC (synchronizing production schedules to achieve reliable, on-time order delivery); Quality Audits (setting up zero-defect standards, in-process inspections, and structured corrective actions); Wastage & Yield Optimization (recovering raw material losses, reducing scrap, and maximizing finished product yield); Food Safety & Compliance (comprehensive regulatory and licensing guidance for FSSAI, FDA, ISO 22000, and HACCP standards across Gujarat, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Pan-India); and MIS & Performance Dashboards (setting up automated daily production reports and live machine efficiency tracking).

As recipients of several prestigious awards in the processing and packaging industry, our seasoned team of engineers and technical advisors brings extensive shop-floor experience across technologies such as extrusion, dehydration, pulping, concentration, retort processing, freezing, and modern automated packaging lines. We work directly on the operational floor to implement actionable systems that drive long-term profitability and sustainable growth.
